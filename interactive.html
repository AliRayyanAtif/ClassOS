How the plan is made

  • Tasks with the closest deadline get your hours first.
  • Work is scheduled to finish the day before each due date, so you have a buffer.
  • Days off are skipped.
  • If a task can’t fit, you get a warning with how many hours you are short.

Hour estimates start at: Assignment 4h · Quiz 3h · Project 8h · Presentation 5h · Other 2h. Change them to match your own speed.
