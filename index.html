How it works

  1. Add your courses: open Add → Class task and use “Manage courses” to list your subjects.
  2. Log every task: title, type, due date and any notes from your teacher.
  3. Check the dashboard: overdue work turns red, this week’s work turns amber.
  4. Generate a study plan: the Planner spreads your hours across the days before each deadline.
  5. Tick it off: mark tasks as done and watch the list shrink.
  • No account or password
  • Saved in your browser’s local storage
  • Works on phone, tablet and laptop
  • Export or clear your data any time
