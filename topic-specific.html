Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

In this browser’s cookies, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded. If you clear your cookies or switch browsers, your list starts empty. Use “Export my data” on the Feedback page to keep a backup.

Can my classmates see my tasks?

No. Each browser keeps its own private copy. To share, export your data or send a classmate the task details yourself.

How does the study planner decide what to do first?

It uses “earliest deadline first”: each day, your hours go to the task that is due soonest, and work is planned to finish the day before the due date.

Was AI used to make this website?

Yes. Some text was drafted with AI help. Our team reviewed it, checked facts against reliable sources, and kept the advice general. Always follow your own teacher’s instructions first.
